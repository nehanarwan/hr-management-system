Set up your company in one minute

Creating a company gives you an administrator account. From there you add departments, employees and their leave entitlements.

  • Your company's data is completely separate from every other company's
  • You become the first administrator
  • A default leave policy is created: 20 days, weekends and holidays not counted
  • Add your team afterwards from the Employees page
